- **A project can word its statuses its own way (AGEND-332).** On a project's page, Statuses opens a dialog where each of the six statuses gets its own name, a colour from the palette, a place in the order, a one-line meaning (shown when you hover it), and Blocked, To confirm and Done may hide their column while it is empty. Home has Default statuses for cards in no project and for projects that have none of their own. The board, the list, the filters, the menus, the card's page and its history, the statistics and the public link all use the project's words and colours. It changes how a status looks, not what it does: a card is still one of the six statuses, and who is told, what a move needs written, the progress numbers and the dependencies are as before. Only a signed-in person can save; a project that never opens the dialog is unchanged, and an upgrade writes nothing. An older release ignores the new tables and shows the built-in names (`docs/downgrade-compatibility.md`).
