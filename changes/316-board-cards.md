section: Changed
---
- **The task board's cards are wider, calmer and show who is working (AGEND-316).** The board and the list fill the page instead of stopping at 1240 px (in the list the title takes the extra width), and a column is 272–360 px wide however many statuses there are (more than fit scroll sideways), so a title no longer breaks onto a third line; a card is as tall as its content, with the title at two lines and one line of status at most (cut with "…", the whole text on hover). The status line now says one thing, in order: why it is stuck, whom it waits for, what is running, what is next, the sub tasks' progress. The coloured bar on the left edge is gone, in every status. The assignee's avatar on a card and in the list is now a button: it opens the same picker as the card's page and the choice saves at once (a card changed meanwhile says so); pointing at it shows the instance's state, what it is doing, how many cards it holds, whether it leads the project and who dispatched the card. Over a read-only link the avatar only shows. On a card's page, the activity — its heading and filter, the timeline and the comment box — is now one panel, set apart from the sub tasks. Layout and style only: nothing about the API, the stored data or the public link's rules changed.
